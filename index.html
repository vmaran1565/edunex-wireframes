Eight design directions

These are static wireframes for discussion. Each of the eight designs shows the same four screens with the same sample data, so you can compare them side by side: Dashboard, Timetable, eRPH and Messages. Click the navigation inside each design to switch screens. Every layout also works on a phone.

Modern designs

Clean, app-style layouts.

Design 1

Slate

A professional admin console. It has a fixed sidebar, a row of key figures, and dense tables with approve and refer-back actions next to each lesson plan. Best for head masters and admins who work in the app all day. Also shows the planned AI features.
