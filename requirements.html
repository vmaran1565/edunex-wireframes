1 Overview

EduNex v2 is one online portal for running a Malaysian primary school. It brings lesson plans, timetables, relief duty, attendance, student affairs, co-curricular work, meetings and school reports into one place, so teachers enter information once and find it again easily.

ProductEduNex v2, the upgrade of Tamilini. Tagline: Lightening Teachers' Workloads.
Schools servedSK, SJK(C) and SJK(T) primary schools, including preschool classes
UsersSchool staff and the EduNex support team. Pupils and parents do not sign in.
DevicesMobile-first web application (PWA) for computer, tablet and phone
LanguagesMalay (default), English, Tamil and Chinese for screen labels
DeliveryThree phases (section 3). Delivery dates, prices and commercial terms are not covered in this document.
AI featuresOptional additional subscription. Every module works without AI (section 4).

A generic product for many schools

EduNex is one standard application shared by many schools. It is not built for a single school or customer, and every school receives the same product and the same updates.

  • Separate data: each school has its own private space. One school can never see another school's data.
  • Configured, not customised: each school sets its own identity and logo, school type, calendar, operating days, time slots, staff, classes, delegated approvers, document templates, deadlines, reminders and subscription plan.
  • No fixed local assumptions: operating days, lesson lengths and school rules are settings. Monday to Friday, a set number of periods or one school's practice are never built in.
  • Changes for one school: a request that only one school needs becomes a setting for all schools or a separate change request. It does not create a separate version of EduNex.

How this document relates to the specifications

This document summarises the phases, inclusions, exclusions, acceptance criteria and AI subscription model. It does not replace the module specifications or the SRS, which remain the detailed baseline. Where they differ from this summary, they apply unless a change request says otherwise.

3 Delivery in three phases

Each phase builds on the one before it and gives schools something usable on its own. Phase 1 creates the school, its people and daily teaching work. Phase 2 adds pupil, staff and meeting operations. Phase 3 joins everything into one view and adds the Question Bank.

PhaseThemeModules deliveredWhat schools can do at the end
Phase 1Foundation and daily teachingM0 Access and billing; Home and Settings; M1 Master Setup; M2 Timetable and Smart Relief; M3 eRPH; M4 Preschool eRPHRegister a school, set it up, publish the timetable, assign relief, and plan, reflect on and review lessons with syllabus progress
Phase 2School operationsM8 Attendance and Student Affairs; M9 eGerak; M7 Meetings; M5 Co-curricularRun registers, HEM services, staff movement, minutes with follow-up actions, and co-curricular records with PAJSK preparation
Phase 3Integration and insightM6 Curriculum, Panels and Presentation; M10 Integrated Dashboard, Task Centre and Document Centre; M11 Question Bank (teacher workspace)Present the school's progress from one place, see all tasks and documents together, and build practice and exam papers from reviewed questions

Why this order

  • Master Setup supplies the school, staff, classes, pupils, calendar and templates that every later module reuses, so it comes first.
  • eRPH needs the timetable and curriculum, and Smart Relief needs the timetable. These belong together in Phase 1.
  • Phase 1 relief uses absences recorded by authorised staff. M9 in Phase 2 adds teacher requests, approvals and actual movement to the same relief engine.
  • M7 owns all meetings and actions, and M5 sets up the shared programme documents. M6 reuses both, so it follows them.
  • M10 only combines records from other modules, so it comes last. M11 links to the panel hub in M6.

What applies to every phase

  • A phase is complete when its modules pass their acceptance criteria in section 5, 6 or 7, plus the shared criteria in section 8, and the customer accepts the phase.
  • Apps from a later phase stay hidden or locked until they are delivered.
  • AI features arrive in the same phase as their module, for schools that subscribe (section 4).
  • The sequence is the plan. Delivery dates for each phase are agreed separately.

4 AI features: additional subscription

AI tools inside EduNex are an optional add-on, charged in addition to the standard EduNex subscription. Every module works fully without AI, and a school can use EduNex without ever buying the add-on.

PhaseRefAI feature in the add-onWithout the add-on
1M1Extract school-plan documents into draft eventsTemplate or manual entry
1M2Read a timetable from a phone photo, image or PDFManual timetable entry
1M3Draft objectives, success criteria, activities and variantsTeacher writes the lesson plan
1M4Suggest ideas for each preschool daily blockTeacher writes the daily plan
2M5Draft weekly, competition reports and proposalsTeacher writes them from templates
2M7Draft minutes and briefing notes from recorder notesRecorder writes the minutes
2M8Draft HEM programme proposals and reportsTeacher writes them from templates
3M6Build slide drafts from school recordsPresentation Mode, manual slides and exports remain
3M11Draft original questions, answers, distractors and rubricsTeachers write questions and use the bank

Rules for every AI feature

  • People stay responsible: a teacher previews and accepts every suggestion. AI cannot submit, approve, sign, publish, close an action or mark a lesson taught.
  • No invented facts: AI cannot create attendance, results, reflections, costs or approvals. Missing facts are flagged for the teacher.
  • Private data stays out: pupil names and identifiers, guardian contacts, health, counselling, discipline and household records, credentials and signatures are not sent to AI.
  • Partial use: a teacher can generate one section, and regenerating it keeps their other edits.
  • Traceable: each use records the person, time, provider, model and whether it was applied.
  • Safe failure: if AI fails, or the school's allowance runs out, manual work and saving carry on.

How the subscription works

  • The add-on is a separate item on the school's subscription, and turning it on or off is a billing setting. AI buttons are locked when it is not active.
  • The add-on includes a usage allowance. The plan level, price and allowance are set out in the commercial terms.
  • Removing the add-on never removes records or drafts already saved, including text produced by AI.

5 Phase 1 modules

Foundation and daily teaching. "AI" marks a feature that needs the additional subscription.

M0 Landing, Registration and Authentication

Included

  • Public landing page with features, pricing, demo and contact; language and school-type selection. Demo uses isolated made-up data.
  • School registration through a SuperAdmin QR or link: 10 proposed fields, application status, and states Draft, Submitted, Needs Information, Approved, Rejected, Inactive.
  • Teacher joining by QR or email invitation with school approval. New teachers must prove an official @moe-dl.edu.my address.
  • Google (DELIMa) and manual sign-in, remembered school and Change School. One account for life, so changing school keeps the person.
  • Password recovery by emailed code. Proposed: 6 digits, 10 minutes, 5 attempts, 60-second resend.
  • Named delegated duties, teacher transfer between schools, and Headteacher succession.
  • Individual teacher and school or group plans, pay-later arrangements, invoices, payment proof, receipts and reminders. Requested options: QR or Touch 'n Go, bank transfer, institutional payment.
  • Background subscription check every 45 seconds. School branding with a small "Powered by EduNex" mark.

Not included

  • Parent login, or any use of a Google password
  • Blocking ordinary work for late payment or a failed status check
  • Automatic school or teacher approval from a payment or QR scan
  • Any official DELIMa integration agreement or KPM endorsement

Acceptance criteria

  • M0-A1: A QR or invitation gives no access until the correct separate approval. Replaying it does not create duplicates or raise privileges.
  • M0-A2: A new teacher without a verified @moe-dl.edu.my identity cannot register.
  • M0-A3: Switching school shows only that school's records. Matching email text alone cannot link or take over an account.
  • M0-A4: Expired or used codes and excess attempts fail. A password reset never grants school access.
  • M0-A5: Overdue payment or a failed check never interrupts authorised work.
  • M0-A6: Payment proof alone does not create a confirmed receipt, and a repeated gateway event does not duplicate a payment.
  • M0-A7: The demo cannot read real schools, send live messages or take payments.

Home, Settings and Notifications

From the SRS and the approved design.

Included

  • Header with school identity, language switch, search, Apps, notifications and account menu; main tabs for Home, Timetable, eRPH, Messages (see section 10) and Syllabus.
  • Home page with welcome card, four number tiles and role-filtered widgets. Computer and phone layouts are saved separately.
  • App launcher: apps outside a school's plan show a padlock and explain why.
  • In-app notifications with filters and links to the record. Email and WhatsApp channels are optional per person.
  • Malaysia, Blue, Green and Dark themes, background scenes, custom colour and school photo.
  • Profile, language, notification channels, privacy and devices, and linked accounts.
  • Installable to a phone home screen, with a simple offline page.

Not included

  • Offline editing or offline approvals
  • Translating records written by users

Acceptance criteria

  • SH-A1: Widgets show only what the person's role allows, and adding one never reveals extra data.
  • SH-A2: Changing the home layout on a phone does not change the computer layout.
  • SH-A3: Theme and language changes apply at once and never change records.
  • SH-A4: Screens work from 1440 px to 390 px wide with no sideways scrolling.
  • SH-A5: Sign-out clears personal items from the device.

M1 Master Setup

Included

  • Five tabs: School Profile; Calendar and Operating Times with School Planning; Staff; Classes and Shared Pupil Registry; Curriculum (DSKP and RPT).
  • School profile by official school code, with Malay and mother-tongue names and logo.
  • School plan by template or manual entry, with one authoritative event per real event. AI: extraction from uploaded documents.
  • Programme reminders 30 and 7 days before, on the day and 7 days after. Meeting notices 2 weeks before.
  • Staff approval, groups, reviewers and appointments with dates, appointing authority and substitute.
  • One shared pupil registry for Years 1 to 6 and Preschool, with stable pupil IDs. Total Pupils = Islamic Education + Moral Education.
  • Master curriculum and the school's RPT; two measures of teaching progress with eRPH (see M3).
  • Guided setup checklist, Excel and CSV import for staff, classes and pupils, and data-quality checks.
  • Reviewed annual rollover, calendar conflict and impact checks, change history and handover.
  • Versioned template library (letterhead, reports, proposals, letters) with unique document reference numbers.

Not included

  • Automatic pupil promotion without a reviewed mapping
  • Copying results, signatures or last year's dates in rollover
  • Schools editing the official DSKP
  • Automatic mapping of operating groups to states, or a claim of KPM endorsement

Acceptance criteria

  • M1-A1: Imports flag unclear data and unmatched teachers. Repeating an import creates no duplicates.
  • M1-A2: Confirmed events create tasks in the right units. Programmes send the four reminders, and meeting notices keep the 2-week rule.
  • M1-A3: Postponing records a reason and replaces unsent reminders; cancelling stops them. A delivered message never closes a task.
  • M1-A4: Total pupils equals Islamic plus Moral, remedial pupils are not added twice, and unclassified data stays flagged.
  • M1-A5: Class changes and promotion keep attendance, membership and earlier evidence.
  • M1-A6: Rollover keeps source records and does not renew privileged appointments.
  • M1-A7: An appointment label alone gives no approval rights across modules.
  • M1-A8: A template update leaves issued documents unchanged, and issued reference numbers stay unique.

M2 Timetable and Smart Relief

Included

  • Time slots, teaching assignments and dated timetable versions, with weekly views by teacher and class, and a teacher's week on a phone.
  • Combined classes and multiple sessions. Clash checks for teachers and classes.
  • Publishing by the Headteacher or GPK Pentadbiran only.
  • AI: read a timetable from a phone photo, image or PDF, including aSc exports, with review beside the source before publishing.
  • Teacher absences recorded by authorised staff: full-day, part-day and multi-day, with configurable reasons.
  • Dual-Mode relief: automatic allocation by fair rotation, or an authorised manual choice under the same checks.
  • Default cap of six teaching periods a day (own plus relief), which a school can change with an audit trail.
  • School-branded relief slip and immediate WhatsApp notice after confirmation; teacher acknowledgement and completion.
  • Timetable, relief and workload reports in PDF, Word or spreadsheet.

Not included

  • Native aSc project files or a lossless aSc connection
  • Teacher self-service absence requests (M9, Phase 2)
  • Automatic split-period relief
  • Assuming a school event cancels all lessons

Acceptance criteria

  • M2-A1: A clash is blocked. An allowed combined or shared lesson is recorded consistently.
  • M2-A2: Only the Headteacher or GPK Pentadbiran can publish, by any route.
  • M2-A3: Changing a timetable never overwrites drafts, submitted or approved eRPH, or completed relief.
  • M2-A4: A partial absence affects only overlapping lessons. Holidays and recess create no relief need.
  • M2-A5: With the default cap, four own periods allow two relief periods, five allow one and six allow none. A double period counts as two.
  • M2-A6: Absent or clashing teachers are excluded, and panel priority never overrides the cap. With no eligible teacher, the lesson stays Unfilled.
  • M2-A7: One confirmation creates one slip and one notice; a change updates both teachers. Slips and notices exclude private absence reasons.
  • M2-A8: An imported draft cannot publish, assign relief or send messages. Uncertain cells stay flagged, never guessed.

M3 Core eRPH

Included

  • Six-section lesson plan: basic information and attendance; DSKP selection; objectives and success criteria; starter, main and plenary; EMK, values, KBAT, i-Think, aids and PBD; reflection and postponement.
  • Curriculum selection by Theme, Unit, Topic, SK and SP, with full Tamil, Chinese and Jawi support.
  • Saving separate from submitting. Submitting the reflection locks all six sections for everyone.
  • Review by Headteacher-appointed reviewers with named accounts, including batch review. No self-approval.
  • Amendments authorised by a reviewer create a new version; the original stays locked.
  • Saturday Replacement, Clone Past eRPH, and Carry Forward of postponed lessons.
  • Two progress measures, side by side: teacher-confirmed SP taught, and SP supported by approved eRPH.
  • Inspection passes for Nazir, PPD and JPN: Headteacher only, time-limited, scoped and read-only, with every view logged.
  • Reminders for plans, reflections and reviews. Individual, daily and weekly PDF and Word exports.
  • AI: draft planning content with teacher review and partial regeneration. Reusable templates need no AI.

Not included

  • Secondary school curriculum (KSSM)
  • A claim of full MOE compliance or official certification
  • Marking a future lesson as taught to complete reflections early
  • Inspector access to pupil identities or private staff details, or any editing

Acceptance criteria

  • M3-A1: Drafts save incomplete work, and submitting rejects missing required fields.
  • M3-A2: After the reflection, no route, including administrator tools, can edit the six sections.
  • M3-A3: Only a current in-scope non-author reviewer can approve or authorise an amendment. The original stays unchanged.
  • M3-A4: Present 28 and achieved 23 gives 5 needing support. Invalid counts are rejected, and zero attendance makes no false claim.
  • M3-A5: A Saturday following Tuesday uses the right schedule and leaves other Tuesdays unchanged.
  • M3-A6: Cloning and carrying forward copy planning only, never attendance, reflection or approval.
  • M3-A7: With 40 target SP, 30 teacher-confirmed and 24 approved, EduNex shows 75% and 60%. Repeated SP never raise the count.
  • M3-A8: An expired or revoked pass fails for records, assets and exports.
  • M3-A9: Tamil, Chinese and Jawi display correctly in forms and exports.

M4 Preschool eRPH

Included

  • Five-section daily plan: routine and morning conversation; physical activity and free play; early literacy and languages; integrated learning; closure and reflection.
  • Selected automatically from the class level, on a versioned preschool curriculum, not one fixed framework.
  • Aggregate attendance, confirmed by the teacher. No pupil names needed to plan.
  • Activity reflections stay editable. Only Submit Final Daily Reflection locks the whole day.
  • Partial days are recorded accurately, block by block.
  • The M3 review, amendment, progress, cloning, relief, inspection and export rules apply.
  • AI: suggestions for each daily block, with teacher review.

Not included

  • Individual developmental assessment, attainment levels and evidence photos
  • Parent portal, parent messaging or pupil-photo sharing
  • A fixed universal daily timetable for preschool

Acceptance criteria

  • M4-A1: The form keeps routine, songs, physical and free play, literacy, integrated learning and closure.
  • M4-A2: Saving an activity reflection leaves the day editable. Final submission locks every section.
  • M4-A3: A partly taught day counts only the SP confirmed as delivered.
  • M4-A4: Publishing a newer curriculum leaves old revisions unchanged.
  • M4-A5: Inspection passes do not expose pupil assessment or photos. Assessment screens stay disabled.

6 Phase 2 modules

School operations. These modules reuse the staff, pupils, calendar, templates and approvals delivered in Phase 1.

M8 Attendance and Student Affairs (HEM)

Included

  • Daily register for Years 1 to 6 and Preschool, validated by the Class Teacher. Register reminders go to the Class Teacher and the coordinator or PK HEM.
  • Absence evidence recorded by teachers on behalf of guardians, with decisions per date.
  • Attendance follow-up alerts and draft warning letters under versioned, validated rules.
  • Discipline cases, one-way guardian notices after verification, and restricted counselling records.
  • RMT, assistance and welfare, SPBT stock and loans, health, safety and HEM programmes.
  • Formal documents: Teacher, then PK HEM, then Headteacher. Corrections keep the original.
  • Safety and programme actions verified by PK HEM or an appointed officer.
  • HEM readiness dashboard and exports. A suggested attendance count for eRPH, confirmed by the teacher.
  • AI: drafts of HEM programme proposals and reports only.

Not included

  • Parent accounts or online excuse submission
  • Automatic expulsion, enrolment removal or punishment
  • APDM, SSDM or government claim connections
  • AI on counselling, health, discipline or warning decisions
  • Medical diagnosis, or use as an emergency response service

Acceptance criteria

  • M8-A1: Unknown attendance never becomes Present or Absent automatically, and totals reconcile to school days.
  • M8-A2: Daily attendance does not wait in the approval queue. A late register sends a reminder, never a guardian warning.
  • M8-A3: Accepted evidence updates only its dates and flags draft letters that are out of date.
  • M8-A4: Unvalidated rules cannot issue letters, and no counter removes a pupil.
  • M8-A5: Formal reports need PK HEM review and Headteacher confirmation, by non-authors.
  • M8-A6: Counselling, medical and financial details stay out of dashboards, messages, AI and slides, and views are logged.
  • M8-A7: Meals, welfare and textbook stock reconcile to recorded movements.

M9 eGerak Staff Movement

Included

  • Full-day absence, late arrival, early departure, temporary exit and urgent recording, with proxy entry.
  • Approval by the Headteacher and PK specifically designated by the Headteacher.
  • Timetable impact, with one coverage need for each affected lesson, feeding the M2 relief engine.
  • Any overlap with a lesson requires cover for the whole period.
  • Emergency cover can be confirmed while the leave decision is pending.
  • Teachers record their own actual exit, arrival and return; authorised administrators review or correct them.
  • Daily movement lists, unfilled cover, workload and operational dashboard.

Not included

  • Leave entitlement, payroll or HRMIS connection
  • Guard accounts, public check-in pages, GPS or biometric attendance
  • Automatic split-period relief, or a second relief engine

Acceptance criteria

  • M9-A1: A draft or pending ordinary request never sends relief. Approved requests allocate cover automatically.
  • M9-A2: Partial overlap produces cover for the whole period. Touching a lesson's edge produces none.
  • M9-A3: Several absences or retries cannot create more than one coverage need for a lesson.
  • M9-A4: Missing timetable data shows as unresolved, not as "no classes affected".
  • M9-A5: Corrections keep the original value, person, time and reason. A recorded exit never approves leave.
  • M9-A6: Private reasons and medical files stay out of relief notices, dashboards and slides.

M7 Meetings, Briefings and Follow-up Actions

Included

  • Staff, management and special meetings, plus short briefings with a simpler format. One record per meeting.
  • Agendas, invitations, attendance including guests, attachments and minutes.
  • Route: recorder submits, Headteacher confirms, with no other stage.
  • Confirmation automatically publishes to the configured internal staff group and sends out the actions.
  • Action closure only after the Headteacher or an appointed officer verifies the evidence.
  • Unfinished actions carry forward as the same action. Corrections create a new version.
  • Action dashboard, meeting register, and PDF and Word exports.
  • AI: minutes and briefing drafts from notes and readable documents, verified by the recorder.

Not included

  • Voice recording, audio transcription or live meeting capture
  • Publishing on the public internet
  • Tasks issued directly from AI output

Acceptance criteria

  • M7-A1: A record starts as Draft and cannot publish before Headteacher confirmation. Recorders cannot confirm their own minutes.
  • M7-A2: Confirmation publishes the exact version to the configured audience. Retrying creates no duplicates.
  • M7-A3: Attendance separates missing entries, apologies and actual participation.
  • M7-A4: Teacher submission does not close an action. Only an in-scope verifier can, and not for their own work.
  • M7-A5: Failed reminders never undo publication or complete work.
  • M7-A6: Corrected minutes keep the original and keep the identity of unchanged actions.

M5 Co-curricular

Included

  • Uniformed bodies, clubs and sports units, advisors, and Year 4 to 6 membership with positions.
  • Annual plans, tracking 12 meetings and 24 hours as separate measures.
  • Weekly logs with attendance, optional pupil QR, and 1 to 4 photos.
  • Proposals, preparation letters, implementation, competition and financial reports, and minutes.
  • Route: Teacher, then PK Kokurikulum, then Headteacher, for reports and annual PAJSK packs.
  • Achievements and extra-curricular evidence, with a versioned PAJSK working calculation of six elements.
  • SEGAK and BMI records.
  • Readiness dashboard, annual logbooks, batch exports and data prepared for SPPB.
  • AI: drafts of weekly reports, competition reports and proposals.

Not included

  • A certified PAJSK calculator or official KPM slips
  • A direct SPPB connection, or storing KPM passwords
  • Exercise or medical advice
  • AI analysis of activity photos
  • Younger-pupil BMI workflows until eligibility is validated

Acceptance criteria

  • M5-A1: Excused, unexcused and unknown attendance stay distinct. Repeated QR scans do not duplicate attendance.
  • M5-A2: The meeting count and annual hours differ correctly, and short or cancelled sessions gain no false credit.
  • M5-A3: PK review alone cannot confirm, and authors cannot review their own reports. Material edits restart review.
  • M5-A4: Financial totals reconcile to transactions and receipts.
  • M5-A5: Results apply only to pupils who took part, not to the whole unit.
  • M5-A6: PAJSK shows "Rules pending validation" and cannot finalise until rule tables are validated. Missing data is not scored as zero.
  • M5-A7: A finalised pack is never reported as saved in SPPB.
  • M5-A8: BMI unit conversions are correct, and incomplete fitness records stay incomplete.

7 Phase 3 modules

Integration and insight. These modules combine what earlier phases record, so they cannot be finished before those modules exist.

M6 Curriculum, Subject Panels and Presentation

Included

  • Annual panels and a six-folder hub: organisation, curriculum, meetings, strategic planning, improvement programmes, question bank and assessment.
  • Curriculum committee meetings through M7, with no fictitious panel.
  • Strategic plans, interventions, PInTaS and OPPM, and programme documents.
  • Panel report route: Teacher or Secretary, Panel Head, PK Administration, Headteacher.
  • Aggregate PBD and UASA analysis from uploaded evidence, after a person validates it.
  • Curriculum dashboard using the two M3 progress measures.
  • Officer Presentation Mode with frozen reporting snapshots.
  • Formal presentations: PK Administration reviews, Headteacher confirms the exact version. PowerPoint and PDF export.
  • AI: slide builder, with a source record for each factual slide.

Not included

  • Pupil-by-pupil PBD or UASA entry
  • Pulling HEM, health or private pupil data into slides
  • Use of eRPH inspection passes for panel records

Acceptance criteria

  • M6-A1: A panel report cannot skip Panel Head or PK review. Authors and out-of-date versions are blocked.
  • M6-A2: All six folders are searchable, and new versions keep approved evidence.
  • M6-A3: Filing a document in a folder never counts as approval.
  • M6-A4: Figures show scope, numerator, denominator and missing data, and shared programmes are not double-counted.
  • M6-A5: Imported analysis needs validated mapping before it can publish.
  • M6-A6: Issued decks stay reproducible, and source changes block confirmation until reconciled.
  • M6-A7: Draft slides are visibly Draft and carry no confirmation marks.

M10 Integrated Dashboard, Task Centre and Document Centre

Included

  • School Dashboard with role-based summaries and drill-down to source records.
  • Task Centre: My Tasks, Team, Waiting for Others, Review Required, Corrections, Upcoming and Overdue.
  • Unified review inbox that keeps each module's approval route.
  • Document Centre: permission-checked search linked to authoritative versions.
  • Reporting packs with a list of contents, and school-branded exports.
  • Notification overview with delivery outcomes. Help and guides.
  • Versioned metric definitions, with stale data shown as Stale, never as zero.

Not included

  • A second database of pupils, timetables or tasks, or editing outside the owning module
  • A global AI assistant, predictive scoring or automatic decisions
  • Public sharing, broad guardian groups or external guest links

Acceptance criteria

  • M10-A1: Filters, search and packs never reveal another school's records, or restricted filenames and counts.
  • M10-A2: Dashboard totals reconcile to authorised records, with cutoff and definition shown.

Submitting the reflection locks the plan. A reviewer approves it or refers it back, and corrections always create a new version.

M2, M9 · Phases 1 and 2

Approved absences trigger automatic relief. No teacher goes over the school cap, which is six periods by default, and an unfilled lesson stays visible.

Flow 6: Daily attendance and follow-up

M8 · Phase 2

The Class Teacher validates the register without an approval queue. Reaching a validated threshold only creates a draft letter, which people review before it is issued.

Flow 7: A meeting, its minutes and follow-up actions

M7 · Phase 2

Headteacher confirmation publishes the minutes automatically to the internal audience. An action closes only after someone verifies its evidence.

Flow 8: Who approves each document

M3, M5, M6, M7, M8, M9 · All phases

Each document type keeps its own route. Nobody approves their own work, and a material change restarts the review.

Flow 9: From a question to an exam paper

M11 · Phase 3

Questions enter the shared bank only after a named non-author reviews them. Teachers build sets from reviewed questions, and any shortfall is shown.

Flow 10: Using an AI feature

AI add-on · All phases

Without the add-on, or when the allowance runs out, manual work carries on. AI never submits anything, so its drafts follow the normal route.
